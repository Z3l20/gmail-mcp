Privacy Policy

Gmail MCP (personal), last updated 9 October 2026

Scope of this policy

Gmail MCP (personal) is a self-hosted application operated by a single individual for their own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its operator.

Google user data accessed

The application has two OAuth clients. The Gmail client requests one Google data scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly: read-only access to Gmail messages, threads, labels and settings. It cannot send, modify or delete mail.

A separate Drive and Calendar client requests:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive, drive.readonly and drive.file: access to the operator's Google Drive files, including creating and editing them.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar, calendar.events and calendar.readonly: access to the operator's calendars and events, including creating and editing them.

Both clients also request openid, email and profile to identify the signed-in account.

How the data is used

Data is read on demand so that the operator, and the AI assistants the operator runs on their own computers, can search, triage and summarise the operator's own mail, files and calendar.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored on the operator's own computers in directories readable only by the operator's user account (file mode 0600). No message content is stored in any database. Revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and deleting the local token files removes all access.

Sharing and transfer

When the operator asks an AI assistant about their mail, files or calendar, the data that assistant reads is sent to the AI model provider that runs it, such as Anthropic, to answer that request. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not otherwise transferred. There is no hosted backend, no analytics and no telemetry.

Limited Use disclosure

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
